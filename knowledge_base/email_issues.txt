IT HELP DESK KNOWLEDGE BASE
Category: Email Issues

Problem: User cannot send or receive emails

Possible Causes:
1. Internet connection problem
2. Incorrect email account configuration
3. Mailbox storage limit reached
4. Email server temporarily unavailable
5. Incorrect username or password
6. Email application synchronization problem

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check that the computer has an active internet connection.
2. Verify that the correct email account is configured.
3. Check whether the mailbox storage limit has been reached.
4. Restart the email application and try again.
5. Sign out and sign in again to the email account.
6. Check whether the email service is experiencing a temporary outage.
7. Verify that the email account password is correct.
8. Try accessing the email account through the webmail service.

Resolution:
Checking the internet connection, restarting the email application, correcting account settings, or clearing mailbox storage may resolve the email issue.

Escalation:
If the user still cannot send or receive emails after troubleshooting, escalate the issue to the IT support team or email administrator.
